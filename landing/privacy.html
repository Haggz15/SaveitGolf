Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026 · Last Updated: July 27, 2026

1. Introduction

SaveitGolf LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the SaveitGolf mobile application and website at saveitgolf.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use our App.

By using SaveitGolf you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: name, email address, username, profile photo, and password when you register
  • Profile information: handicap index, home state, and any other information you choose to add to your profile
  • User Content: photos, videos, scorecards, captions, and comments you post
  • Communications: messages you send to us through support or contact forms

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Location data: GPS coordinates when you use location-based features such as posting content at a golf course or viewing nearby courses. We only collect precise location when you actively use these features.
  • Device information: device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information
  • Usage data: pages and features you access, time spent in the App, links clicked, and other usage patterns
  • Log data: IP address, browser type, referring pages, and error logs

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Golf course data: We use third-party golf course databases to display course information
  • Social login: If you sign in using Apple or Google we receive basic profile information from those services in accordance with their privacy policies
  • Analytics providers: We use analytics tools to understand how users interact with the App

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide and improve the App's features and functionality
  • Display your content to other users as you intend
  • Verify your location for GPS-locked posting features
  • Send you notifications about activity on your posts
  • Respond to your support requests and inquiries
  • Send you updates about the App if you opt in
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Detect and prevent fraud and abuse
  • Analyze usage trends to improve the App

4. How We Share Your Information

4.1 Public Information

Your username, profile photo, posts, scorecards, course rankings, and other content you choose to make public are visible to all users of SaveitGolf and may be indexed by search engines.

4.2 Service Providers

We share information with third-party vendors who help us operate the App including hosting providers, analytics services, mapping services, and customer support tools. These providers are contractually required to protect your information and may only use it to provide services to us.

4.3 Brand Partners and Sponsors

We may share aggregated, anonymized usage data with brand partners and sponsors. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal process such as a court order or subpoena.

4.5 Business Transfers

If SaveitGolf is acquired, merged with, or sold to another company, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your information is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

5. Location Data

SaveitGolf uses your GPS location to:

  • Verify you are physically at a golf course when posting content
  • Show you nearby golf courses on the map
  • Display location-relevant content in your feed

You can control location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location access will limit certain features of the App such as GPS-verified posting and the nearby courses feature.

We do not share your precise real-time location with other users. Your general location (state) may be displayed on your posts as a region tag.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. If you delete your account we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law or for legitimate business purposes such as resolving disputes.

User Content you have posted publicly may remain visible after account deletion if other users have interacted with it. You may request deletion of specific content by contacting us.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal information
  • Portability: Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Opt out: Opt out of marketing communications at any time by following the unsubscribe link in emails or adjusting your notification settings in the App

To exercise these rights contact us at privacy@saveitgolf.com.

8. Children's Privacy

SaveitGolf is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information we will take steps to delete such information. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with their information please contact us at privacy@saveitgolf.com.

9. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encryption of data in transit and at rest, secure server infrastructure, and access controls.

However no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

10. Third-Party Links and Services

The App may contain links to third-party websites or integrate with third-party services such as golf course booking platforms or sporting goods retailers. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete your personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your California privacy rights contact us at privacy@saveitgolf.com.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy in the App or by email. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
