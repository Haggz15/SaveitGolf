Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use SaveitGolf, we collect information you provide directly, such as your email address, username, profile photo, and the photos, videos, captions, and scorecards you post. We also collect information about your usage of the App, and, if you grant permission, your device’s approximate location so we can show nearby courses and tag posts with course locations.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to operate and improve SaveitGolf, to display your posts and profile to other users according to your settings, to power features like the course map and following feed, and to communicate with you about your account.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Your profile and posts are visible to other users of the App according to your settings. We may share information with service providers (such as our hosting and storage providers) who help us operate the App, and as required by law.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account information and content for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You can review and update your profile information within the App, control location permissions through your device settings, and delete posts you’ve shared. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

6. Children’s Privacy

SaveitGolf is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your data rights, contact us at:
saveitgolfapp@gmail.com
