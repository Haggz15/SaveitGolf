Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Open the SaveitGolf app and tap “Sign Up” from the welcome screen. You’ll need a valid email address and can set up your profile, including your username, photo, and home course.

How do I post a round or scorecard?

Tap the “+” button from the main feed to create a new post. You can attach photos or videos, log your scorecard, tag the course you played, and add a caption before sharing it with your followers.

How do I find and add friends?

Use the search icon to look up friends by username, or allow contact/location permissions so SaveitGolf can suggest golfers you may know. Tap “Follow” on any profile to connect.

How do I find a golf course?

Use the map or search tab to browse nearby courses. Tap a course to view details, see posts tagged there, and add it to your rankings or scorecards.

How do I change my privacy settings?

Go to your profile, tap the settings icon, and adjust who can see your posts, follow you, or view your location. You can also manage notification preferences from the same menu.

How do I report a problem or inappropriate content?

Tap the “…” menu on any post or profile and select “Report.” For urgent issues or anything not covered there, email us directly at saveitgolfapp@gmail.com.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account directly in the app. Go to your Profile screen and scroll to the bottom to find the Delete Account option. Tapping it will permanently delete your account and all associated data including posts, scorecards and profile information. This action cannot be undone. See our Privacy Policy for details on how your data is handled.

The app isn’t working correctly — what should I do?

Try closing and reopening the app, and make sure you’re on the latest version from the App Store. If the issue continues, email us at saveitgolfapp@gmail.com with a description of the problem and your device type — screenshots help a lot.

Still need help? Email saveitgolfapp@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you.
